AnuranCall demo (anurancall 1.0.0)
writes to demo/sessions.json — your own ~/.anurancall/sessions.json is untouched

=== 1. an empty log, before any survey

$ python3 anurancall.py --data demo/sessions.json sessions
sessions
  no sessions yet — the log fills in as you record counts
  record your first count with:
    python3 anurancall.py count --species 'Spring Peeper' --site 'Mud Pond' --intervals '1.0, 1.2'
  (leave --intervals off to tap enter once per call)

=== 2. tap mode: press enter once per call (three calls, about half a second apart)
$ { sleep .55; echo; sleep .5; echo; sleep .6; echo; echo q; } | \
    python3 anurancall.py --data demo/sessions.json count --species 'Spring Peeper' \
      --site 'Mud Pond' --temp 16.0 --notes 'chorus from the north bank, water still'
